FAQ

Frequently Asked

What happens if a seller stops responding?

In the event of unresponsiveness from a seller, after receiving payment, our platform offers a resolution process to address such situations and ensure a fair outcome for both parties, You just have to open a dispute on the transaction and we will resolve it.

What happens if a buyer receives my delivery and stops responding?

If a buyer goes silent after receiving a delivery, All you need to do is to mark the transaction as "delivered" and iff the buyer doesn;t respond or open a dispute within 24hours, we will release the funds to you the seller.

What if i receive an order with unmatched details?

If a buyer creates an order with details not matching to your product, then you can request a review/change on the order before accepting the order to avoid issues in case of dispute

How are dispute resolved?

Incase of a dispute, First the Buyer and seller will be joined in a chat room on the app to be able to resolve the issues, and when they are unable to, one of our dispute resolution manager's will come in a check the order details and the evidence available and make a fair conclution.
